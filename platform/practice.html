Financial Assistance for Students

Assistance under this invented programme is offered to enrolled students whose family income falls below the notified ceiling. Applications are verified first by the institute nodal officer and then by the district office. Renewal is required at the start of every academic year, and is not automatic.

Keep ready before beginning: enrolment number, bank account details in the applicant’s own name, an income certificate issued within the last twelve months, and the institute verification code.

Continuing Students

Students who received assistance in the previous academic year must confirm continued enrolment before the closing date. This is a separate process from a fresh application and cannot be completed by re-applying.
