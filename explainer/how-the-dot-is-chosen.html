    Why the second one goes red

    On the withdraw screen two controls score almost the same — the amount field at 0.815 and the confirm button at 0.763. A win by 0.052 is reported as a win by 0.052. The guide still shows the dot; it just stops pretending.

    The colour is not a self-report

    Every model will tell you it is confident. So the band needs two numbers to agree: what the words scored, and what the page itself supports — is the control named, unique, and actually the topmost thing at that point? The percentage shown is the min() of the two, never the average, so the number can never contradict the colour.

    No model was running

    Every figure here came from the offline matcher: a synonym table, no network, no API key. That is why the best it will claim is amber — words alone never earn green. Point it at a model and the same machinery runs with better inputs.
